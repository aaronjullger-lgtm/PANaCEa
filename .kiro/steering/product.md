# PANaCEa - Medical Education Platform

PANaCEa is an AI-powered medical education platform designed for PANCE/PANRE exam preparation. It's a comprehensive learning system that combines spaced repetition, adaptive questioning, and gamification to help physician assistant students and practitioners master medical knowledge.

## Core Features

- **Hybrid Content Engine**: Advanced system that combines AI generation with quality control, reducing costs by 90% while maintaining high-quality medical content
- **Adaptive Learning**: AI-powered question generation targeting user weaknesses and growth areas
- **Spaced Repetition System (SRS)**: Evidence-based review scheduling for long-term retention
- **Multiple Training Modes**: Photo drills, rapid recall, DDx comparison, pharmacology, and specialized clinical scenarios
- **Performance Analytics**: Detailed tracking with heatmaps, progress dashboards, and weakness analysis
- **Gamification**: Achievement system, streaks, and character collection to maintain engagement

## Target Users

- PA students preparing for PANCE
- Practicing PAs preparing for PANRE
- Medical educators and institutions
- Study groups and collaborative learning environments

## Key Value Propositions

1. **Cost-Effective**: 90% reduction in content generation costs through intelligent caching
2. **Quality Controlled**: All AI-generated questions validated before reaching users
3. **Personalized**: Adaptive learning paths based on individual performance patterns
4. **Comprehensive**: Covers all PANCE blueprint topics with appropriate weighting
5. **Evidence-Based**: Uses proven learning science principles (SRS, active recall, metacognition)
