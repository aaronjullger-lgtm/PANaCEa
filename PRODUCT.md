## Users and purpose

PA students preparing for PANCE/PANRE while balancing coursework and clinical rotations. PANaCEa uses behavioral analytics to estimate concept-level recall confidence and schedule review automatically, with the aim of maximizing retention and minimizing study effort. The owner clarified that concepts recur through new question wording rather than repetition of the same item.

## Owner clarification and implementation boundary

The September 23 clarification makes behavioral, concept-level spaced repetition the primary story. The forgetting curve is the explanatory foundation, not one universal schedule. Current production code uses confidence scores and memory stability to adjust scheduling; Wilson confidence bounds are read-only. Do not claim calibrated statistical confidence intervals drive scheduling without verifying a future implementation change. Never-repeat wording is the owner’s intended experience; audit exposure handling per route before a universal guarantee.
