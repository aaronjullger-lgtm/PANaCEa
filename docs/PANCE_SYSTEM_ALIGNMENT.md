## Key Findings & Recommendations

### ⚠️ Critical Gap: Pulmonary System
**Current:** 46 conditions (3.8%)
**Target:** ~110 conditions (9%)
**Gap:** ~64 conditions needed

**Recommended Additions:**
- Asthma variants (exercise-induced, occupational, allergic)
- COPD subtypes (emphysema, chronic bronchitis)
- Pneumonia types (community-acquired, hospital-acquired, aspiration)
- Interstitial lung diseases (IPF, NSIP, COP)
- Occupational lung diseases (asbestosis, silicosis, coal worker's)
- Pleural disorders (empyema, hemothorax, chylothorax)
- Pulmonary vascular (chronic PE, pulmonary HTN variants)
- Sleep disorders (central sleep apnea, obesity hypoventilation)
- Pediatric respiratory (croup, bronchiolitis variants, RSV)

### 📈 Over-Represented Systems
Systems exceeding PANCE targets (not problematic, but noted):

1. **Eyes, Ears, Nose, and Throat** (10.1% vs 6%)
   - Strong HEENT coverage, appropriate for primary care focus
   
2. **Musculoskeletal System** (10.2% vs 8%)
   - Excellent coverage of fractures, arthritis, soft tissue injuries
   
3. **Dermatologic System** (8.1% vs 4%)
   - Comprehensive dermatology coverage

4. **Psychiatry/Behavioral Science** (8.6% vs 7%)
   - Good mental health condition coverage

### ✅ Well-Balanced Systems (±0.5%)
Nine systems within target range:
- Gastrointestinal System/Nutrition
- Infectious Diseases
- Neurologic System
- Hematologic System
- Endocrine System
- Renal System

- Cardiovascular System (slightly under)
